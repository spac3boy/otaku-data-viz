How To Read It

Map Rules

  • Each lane is a fictional continuity. Shared lane means shared history; separate lane usually means independent story world.
  • Solid markers are mainline routes. Dashed markers are supplemental, compilations, side stories, or alternate retellings.
  • The Gundam silhouette marks every animated production; card borders and detail labels identify supplemental works, compilations, and retellings.
  • Color separates continuities, but every production also carries text labels and metadata.

Mode Rules

  • Story order places works by fictional chronology within their continuity when a useful era label exists.
  • Release order places the same works by first real-world release year, revealing how the franchise actually unfolded.
  • Some placements are approximate, disputed, or alternate-continuity-adjacent. Those are labeled in the detail panel.

Methodology And Scope

Scope

This MVP focuses on major animated Gundam productions and major fictional continuities. It intentionally leaves room for manga, novels, games, live-action, short promotional animation, and deeper episode-level arcs later.

    Uncertain Decisions

    Turn A, Reconguista in G, Thunderbolt, The Origin, and Cucuruz Doan's Island are represented with caution where chronology, adaptation status, or meta-continuity relationships can be argued differently.

    The map is a recommendation and orientation tool, not an official canon declaration.
